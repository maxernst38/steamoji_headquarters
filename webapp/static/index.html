Select the field

Drag the four corners onto the field’s floor corners, then line the rings up with the real goals — easier and more accurate than judging faint tile seams. Then press Rotate origin until the red rings sit on the actual red goals. That is what tells the system which alliance is which; get it wrong and every point is credited to the opposing team. Yellow rings are neutral goals, white is the tall centre goal, and the diamond is the Midfield.

Mark the robots

Drag a box around each robot. Motion detection cannot see a robot that is holding still, so correcting these fixes the start of the match — and every box you draw becomes a training label.
