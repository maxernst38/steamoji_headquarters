OPR is what this team contributed to its own alliance's score, DPR what it allowed the opposing alliance, and CCWM the difference. All three are solved across every alliance at that event at once, so they do not depend on who the team was paired with — which win rate and average score both do.

Read them as rankings, not quantities. The fit column is honest about the precision: these explain roughly a third of score variance, and each team's figure rests on about five alliances. DPR can even come out slightly negative, which is the least-squares fit overshooting rather than a team causing negative points.

A team playing pure defense suppresses opponents instead of scoring, so it shows as low OPR and low DPR. That is why CCWM is the column to lead with — at the Mall of America event the undefeated winner ranked 6th on OPR and 1st on CCWM. {% if elo and elo.pool is not none and elo.pool > 0 %} This team's Elo sits in a smaller pool of events that share no teams with the main one, so it is not comparable to Elo elsewhere in the table. {% endif %}
